LOVE PSYCHOLOGY 💞 The Tiny Habit of Couples Who Last (Gottman)

November 14, 2026 · 43-second video · 3 action points · quiz
YouTubeInstagramFacebook
The idea in one line

Couples who last answer each other's small bids for attention, so notice them and turn toward your partner.

Try this today

Check yourself

According to the Gottman Institute, which response to a bid can hurt more?

The lesson

Couples who stayed married answered each other's tiny bids 86 percent of the time. Couples who divorced? Only 33.

Psychologist John Gottman studied newlyweds, then checked back six years later.

A bid is any small try for attention or affection. A smile. Saying your name. Asking for help.

So forget viral loyalty tests. The Gottmans point to small everyday moments instead.

What's a small bid your person makes? Tell me in the comments.
