The Peak-End Rule: Why Endings Matter Most (Kahneman Study)

November 8, 2026 · 40-second video · 3 action points · quiz
YouTubeInstagramFacebook
The idea in one line

We remember experiences by their most intense moment and their ending, not by how long they lasted.

Try this today

Check yourself

In Kahneman's cold-water study, which trial did most people choose to repeat?

The lesson

People chose more pain over less. And it made sense to their brains.

In a 1993 study, Daniel Kahneman's team tested icy water. Sixty seconds, or ninety with a slightly warmer ending.

That's the peak-end rule. We judge an experience by its most intense moment and its ending, not its length.

A 2022 analysis of 174 effect sizes backed it up.
