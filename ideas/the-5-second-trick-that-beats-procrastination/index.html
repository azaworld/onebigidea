The 5-Second Trick That Beats Procrastination

November 7, 2026 · 36-second video · 3 action points · quiz
YouTubeInstagramFacebook
The idea in one line

Count 5-4-3-2-1 and move before your brain talks you out of it.

Try this today

Check yourself

You hesitate on something you know you should do. What does the 5 Second Rule say?

The lesson

You already know what to do. You just don't do it. Here's a five second fix.

It's called the 5 Second Rule, from author and podcaster Mel Robbins.

The moment you feel the urge to do something you know you should, count backwards. Five, four, three, two, one.

Then move. Stand up. Open the laptop. Send the message. Before your mind starts making excuses.

Counting down interrupts the habit of hesitating. It works like a launch signal for your brain.

It won't fix everything. But it's a simple way to start the things you keep putting off.
