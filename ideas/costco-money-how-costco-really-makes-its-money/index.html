COSTCO MONEY 💳 How Costco Really Makes Its Money

November 28, 2026 · 47-second video · 3 action points · quiz
YouTubeInstagramFacebook
The idea in one line

Costco keeps about 3 cents per dollar; its $5.9B in membership fees, earned through loyalty, is the engine.

Use it at work

Check yourself

How many warehouses does Costco run worldwide?

The lesson

Costco made nine point two billion dollars in profit last year. Its membership fees alone brought in five point nine billion.

That's from Costco's fiscal 2026 results, reported in September.

On over three hundred billion dollars in revenue, it keeps only about three cents per dollar as profit.

Low prices keep members coming back, and members pay their fee every year. That's the engine.

The lesson: earn trust first, and repeat customers become your steadiest income.

Would you pay a yearly fee to shop somewhere? Tell me in the comments.
