VENUS FACT 🪐 A Day on Venus Is Longer Than Its Year

November 19, 2026 · 50-second video · 3 action points · quiz
YouTubeInstagramFacebook
The idea in one line

Venus spins so slowly that one day (243 Earth days) lasts longer than its year (225 Earth days).

Key facts

Check yourself

On Venus, how long does it take from sunrise to sunset?

The lesson

One day on Venus lasts 243 Earth days. But its whole year? Only 225.

NASA says Venus spins so slowly, it circles the Sun before it turns around even once.

And it spins backward. So on Venus, the Sun rises in the west and sets in the east.

Under those thick clouds, it's about 467 degrees Celsius. Hot enough to melt lead.

If your day lasted 243 days, what would you do with it? Tell me in the comments.
