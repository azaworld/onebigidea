How to Change Your Life in 90 Days (Jay Shetty's Method)

November 7, 2026 · 37-second video · 3 action points · quiz
YouTubeInstagramFacebook
The idea in one line

Do less, make it fun, and plan if-then rules so new habits last.

Try this today

Check yourself

To build a new habit, should you add more goals, or do less?

The lesson

Most people try to change everything at once. That's exactly why they quit.

On his podcast On Purpose, Jay Shetty shared a ninety day approach to change that lasts.

Then pair a task you avoid with something you enjoy, like your favorite podcast only during workouts.

And plan with if-then rules. If it's seven a.m., then I walk for ten minutes.

What would you change in the next ninety days? Tell me in the comments.

How to change your life in 90 days without burning out: Jay Shetty's approach from his On Purpose podcast. Do less, pair hard tasks with something you enjoy (temptation bundling), and use if-then plans so habits stick. Explained and animated in our own words.
