Why was NASA's Mars Climate Orbiter lost in 1999?

The lesson

NASA lost a 125 million dollar spacecraft because of a units mix-up.

In 1999, the Mars Climate Orbiter was arriving at Mars after a nine month trip.

One piece of software reported thruster data in imperial pounds. The navigation team's software expected metric newtons.

The mismatch was never fixed, and tiny errors kept adding up for months.

The spacecraft came in far too low and was lost at Mars.

The lesson every engineer learns: test the boring stuff. Units, formats, assumptions.
