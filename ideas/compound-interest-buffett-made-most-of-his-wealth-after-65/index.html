COMPOUND INTEREST ⏳ Buffett Made Most of His Wealth After 65

November 18, 2026 · 47-second video · 3 action points · quiz
YouTubeInstagramFacebook
The idea in one line

Buffett's skill was investing, but his secret was time: most of his wealth came after he turned 65.

Try this today

Check yourself

Jim Simons averaged 66% a year, Buffett about 22%. Who ended up richer?

The lesson

Warren Buffett is worth over 140 billion dollars. Almost all of it came after he turned 65.

In 1995, at 65, he had about 12 billion. By 2025, Bloomberg put him near 141 billion.

Author Morgan Housel says about 98 percent arrived after 65. His skill is investing. His secret is time.

That's compounding. Growth stacks on growth, so the biggest jumps come late.
