The lesson

Work that took expert teams years, an AI just did in four weeks.

Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Code mathematically proved key security promises in Signal's encryption code.

WIRED says that protocol also helps protect chats on WhatsApp and Google Messages.

The AI wrote over six million lines of proof, and a checker called Lean verified them.

The real idea is teamwork. Humans chose what to prove and reviewed the tricky parts. AI did the grind.
