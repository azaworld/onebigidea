MAKE FRIENDS 🤝 How Many Hours It Takes to Make a Friend (Study)

November 20, 2026 · 43-second video · 3 action points · quiz
YouTubeInstagramFacebook
The idea in one line

Friendship is built with time: about 50 hours for a casual friend and over 200 for a close one.

Try this today

Check yourself

Which hours count more toward a friendship?

The lesson

It takes about 50 hours together to make a casual friend. A close friend? More than 200.

Professor Jeffrey Hall at the University of Kansas studied adults who had just moved, and new college students.

About 50 hours for a casual friend. About 90 to become a real friend. Over 200 for a close one.

As Hall puts it, you can't make people spend time with you, but you can invite them.
