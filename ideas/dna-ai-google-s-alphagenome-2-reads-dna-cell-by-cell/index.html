DNA AI 🧬 Google's AlphaGenome 2 Reads DNA Cell by Cell

November 24, 2026 · 52-second video · 3 action points · quiz

The idea in one line

Context changes everything: same DNA, different cell, different result. Give AI your context too.

Use it at work

Check yourself

On a test with 28 types of immune cells, did AlphaGenome 2 beat the original AlphaGenome?

The lesson

Google's new AI learned from over 107 million cells to crack one mystery.

Every cell in your body carries the same DNA. So why does a gut cell act nothing like a heart cell?

Google DeepMind's AlphaGenome 2, announced November 11, reads up to a million DNA letters for one cell type at a time.

It's for research only, not diagnosis. But it helps scientists narrow down targets before costly lab work.

The lesson? Context changes everything. Same DNA, different cell, different result.
