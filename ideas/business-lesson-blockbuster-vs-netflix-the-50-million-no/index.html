BUSINESS LESSON 📼 Blockbuster vs Netflix: The $50 Million No

November 14, 2026 · 46-second video · 3 action points · quiz
YouTubeInstagramFacebook
The idea in one line

Blockbuster laughed off Netflix for $50 million; if you won't disrupt yourself, someone else will.

Use it at work

Check yourself

How many Blockbuster stores are left today?

The lesson

In 2000, Netflix offered to sell itself to Blockbuster for 50 million dollars. Blockbuster said no.

Netflix was a tiny DVD-by-mail startup. Blockbuster had about 9,000 stores.

Co-founder Marc Randolph told Fortune that Blockbuster laughed them out of the room.

About ten years later, Blockbuster was bankrupt. Netflix grew to be worth over 150 billion dollars.

Randolph's lesson: if you won't disrupt yourself, someone else will do it for you.

Which company is the next Blockbuster? Tell me in the comments.

Sources
