The lesson

A Hollywood movie star helped invent the tech behind your Wi-Fi. And she never earned a penny from it.

During World War Two, actress Hedy Lamarr teamed up with composer George Antheil.

Their idea: make a radio signal hop between frequencies, so a guided torpedo was hard to jam.

But today, that hopping idea sits underneath Wi-Fi, GPS and Bluetooth.

Credit came late. A Pioneer Award in 1997, and the Inventors Hall of Fame in 2014.
