AI PRODUCTIVITY 🤖 Eliminate Before You Automate (Tim Ferriss)

November 15, 2026 · 45-second video · 3 action points · quiz
YouTubeInstagramFacebook
The idea in one line

AI multiplies whatever you give it, so cut the busywork first and only then automate what's left.

Use it at work

Check yourself

According to Tim Ferriss, which step should come first?

The lesson

Using AI to work faster can actually give you more work. Tim Ferriss explains why.

On The Tim Ferriss Show, episode 887, he called AI tools accelerants. In his words, they make more of whatever is.

So if your day is full of busywork, AI just helps you do more busywork. Faster, but not freer.

His fix is 80/20 thinking. Cut the trivial many, and focus on the critical few that can make a huge difference.

What's one task you would delete instead of automate? Tell me in the comments.
