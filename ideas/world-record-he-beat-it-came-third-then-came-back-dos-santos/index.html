The lesson

At the Tokyo Olympics, Alison dos Santos ran faster than the old world record. And finished third.

Karsten Warholm won in a new record, 45.94. Dos Santos said that loss hurt so bad.

He told himself he never wanted to feel that again.

So his team changed a lot in training and racing. Start faster. Finish stronger.

Zurich, August 27th: 45.80. Warholm, a distant second. This month, it became official.

What near-miss made you come back stronger? Tell me in the comments.
