How to Never Give Up: James Dyson's 5,127 Prototypes

The idea in one line

James Dyson built 5,127 prototypes before one worked: every failure is data for the next try.

How many prototypes did James Dyson build before his vacuum worked?

The lesson

One inventor failed five thousand, one hundred and twenty six times. Then he changed his whole industry.

In 1978, James Dyson wanted a vacuum that never lost suction. So he started building one with no bag.

He went over a million dollars into debt. But every failure taught him something.

In 1993, his own DC01 vacuum finally launched.

What would you try five thousand times for? Tell me in the comments.
