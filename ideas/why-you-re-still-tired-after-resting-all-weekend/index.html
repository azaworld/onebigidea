Why You're Still Tired After Resting All Weekend

November 7, 2026 · 32-second video · 3 action points · quiz
YouTubeInstagramFacebook
The idea in one line

Rest from whatever actually drained you, not just from work in general.

Try this today

Check yourself

Your weekend rest isn't restoring your energy. What might be wrong?

The lesson

You rested all weekend and you're still exhausted. You might be resting from the wrong thing.

On his podcast On Purpose, Jay Shetty shared a simple shift for when rest isn't working.

Rest from whatever actually drained you, not just from work in general.

Drained by screens? Lying on the couch scrolling isn't rest. Get away from the screen.

Drained by people? You may need quiet time alone, not another busy plan.

Match the rest to the drain, and your energy starts coming back.
