The lesson

Maya: This runner had never run a marathon. Then he won New York.

Leo: Wait. His first one ever?

Maya: Yes. On November first, Sweden's Andreas Almgren won it in two hours, six minutes, one second.

Leo: Was it close?

Maya: Thirteen seconds ahead of Italy's Crippa. He's the first Swede ever to win it.

Leo: So he's just naturally gifted?

Maya: Not quite. AP reports a stress fracture wrecked his Paris Olympic hopes. So he quit his job and coached himself.

Leo: And New York was the plan all along?

Maya: He watched it a year earlier and decided that night to debut there.

Leo: So, a setback, a one year plan, and a win on his first try?

Maya: Exactly. Today, pick one big goal, give it a date, and take the first step.

Leo: What's your one year goal? Tell us in the comments.
