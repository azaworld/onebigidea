How to Change Your Life in 90 Days (Jay Shetty's Method)

How to change your life in 90 days without burning out: Jay Shetty's approach from his On Purpose podcast. Do less, pair hard tasks with something you enjoy (temptation bundling), and use if-then plans so habits stick. Explained and animated in our own words.

Why You're Still Tired After Resting All Weekend

On his podcast On Purpose, Jay Shetty suggests that if rest isn't restoring your energy, you may be resting from the wrong thing. Rest from whatever actually drained you, not just from work in general. Explained and animated in our own words.

Turn Your Phone Gray to Scroll Less

Cognitive neuroscientist Dr. Sahar Yousef suggests switching your phone to grayscale. Without bright colors, apps are less stimulating, which can make mindless scrolling easier to resist. Explained and animated in our own words.

Podcast lessons

The 5-Second Trick That Beats Procrastination

The 5 Second Rule, popularized by author and podcaster Mel Robbins: when you feel the urge to act on something you know you should do, count 5-4-3-2-1 and move before you talk yourself out of it. Explained and animated in our own words.
