History & science

Sharks Are Older Than Trees

The earliest shark-like scales in the fossil record date to about 450 million years ago, while the first trees appeared around 385 million years ago. For tens of millions of years, sharks swam in oceans next to land with no forests at all. Modern shark groups came much later, but the shark lineage beat trees by a long way.

Turn Your Phone Gray to Scroll Less

Cognitive neuroscientist Dr. Sahar Yousef suggests switching your phone to grayscale. Without bright colors, apps are less stimulating, which can make mindless scrolling easier to resist. Explained and animated in our own words.

AI & tech

NASA Lost a $125 Million Spacecraft Over Units

In 1999 NASA lost the Mars Climate Orbiter because one piece of ground software produced thruster data in imperial units (pound-force seconds) while the navigation software expected metric units (newton-seconds). The spacecraft flew too low and was lost.

The 5-Second Trick That Beats Procrastination

The 5 Second Rule, popularized by author and podcaster Mel Robbins: when you feel the urge to act on something you know you should do, count 5-4-3-2-1 and move before you talk yourself out of it. Explained and animated in our own words.
